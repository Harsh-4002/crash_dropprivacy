Crash Drop – Privacy Policy

by Qissora · Last updated: 10 November 2026

The short version

  • Crash Drop has no accounts, no login and no sign-up. We do not ask for your name, email, phone number or location.
  • Your game progress (levels, stars, coins, settings) is stored only on your device.
  • The free version shows ads from Google AdMob. AdMob may use your device's advertising ID and similar data to show and measure ads. In the EEA, UK and some other regions you are asked for your choice first.
  • We use Google Firebase (Analytics and Crashlytics) to count how the game is played (for example which levels are too hard) and to find crashes. It uses a random app identifier, not your name or email. Where the law requires it we ask first, and you can switch it off any time in Settings.
  • Purchases (coins, Remove Ads) are handled by Google Play. We never see or store your card details.
  • We do not sell your personal data.
  • Crash Drop is not directed to children under 13.

1. Who we are

Crash Drop (“the game”, “the app”) is an Android game published by Qissora (“we”, “us”). We are the controller of the limited information described in this policy. You can reach us at itsharshkantgupta@gmail.com.

This policy explains what the game stores, which third-party services it uses, and the choices you have. By using the game you agree to it. If you do not agree, please do not use the game.

2. Data stored on your device

To let you continue where you left off, the game saves the following in its private storage on your phone:

  • level progress, stars and best scores (Normal and Hard mode);
  • coins, boosters, unlocked items and equipped cosmetics;
  • your settings (music, sound, vibration and similar options);
  • simple counters such as when the last interstitial ad was shown;
  • the fact that you bought “Remove Ads” or a coin pack, and an identifier of each completed Google Play purchase so that it is never credited twice.

This saved game data stays on your device. We do not run a game server and we do not receive a copy of your save file. A few summary numbers from it (for example the highest level you have reached, your stars and your coin balance) are sent as analytics events, as described in section 4. None of it can be used to identify you.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game is free and is supported by ads. It uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob), provided by Google LLC, to show:

  • rewarded video ads that you choose to watch (for example to continue a level or double your coins), and
  • interstitial ads between some levels (these are removed if you buy “Remove Ads”).

When an ad is requested or shown, Google and its advertising partners may automatically collect and process information from your device, such as:

InformationPurpose
Advertising ID (a resettable device identifier) and device/OS information (model, OS version, language, screen)Selecting and delivering ads, limiting how often an ad is shown, fraud prevention
IP address and approximate location derived from itDelivering ads, security and fraud prevention, regional rules
Ad interactions (impressions, clicks, whether a reward was earned) and diagnosticsMeasuring ad performance, paying for ad views, improving the service

We do not receive this information in a form that identifies you, and we do not combine it with anything else. Google processes it under its own policies: Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Your choices about ads

  • Consent: where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom) the game asks for your choice through Google's consent form before ads are loaded. To see the form again, clear the app's data (see section 9).
  • Advertising ID: on Android you can reset it or opt out of ads personalisation in Settings → Privacy (or Google) → Ads, and you can delete the advertising ID there.
  • Remove Ads: buying it stops interstitial ads. Optional rewarded videos remain available if you choose to watch them.

4. Analytics and crash reports (Google Firebase)

To understand how the game is played and to fix problems, the game uses two services from Google Firebase, provided by Google LLC: Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics.

InformationPurpose
A randomly generated app instance ID and installation ID (they are not your name, email or Advertising ID, and they change if you reinstall the game)Counting players and sessions, and recognising a returning player without knowing who they are
Device and app information: phone model, Android version, language, app version, and a country or region derived from your IP addressUnderstanding which devices the game must run well on, and where it is played

We use Firebase for measuring how the game is played and for crash reports only. Its advertising features (such as ad personalisation signals) are switched off in the game. We do not use this information to identify you, and we do not combine it with your name, email, contacts or any other personal information, because we do not have any. Google processes it under its own terms: Privacy and Security in Firebase.

Your choices

  • Consent: where the law requires it (for example in the EEA and the UK) nothing is collected until you agree in the consent form shown when you first open the game. If you do not agree, no analytics or crash data is sent.
  • Switch it off any time: turn off Share anonymous usage data in the game's Settings. From then on no analytics or crash data is sent.
  • Advertising choices are separate (see section 3).

5. Purchases (Google Play)

In-app purchases (coin packs and “Remove Ads”) are processed entirely by Google Play using the Google Play Billing Library. Google handles your payment details under the Google Privacy Policy and Google Play's terms. We never see or store your card number or other payment credentials.

The game receives only a purchase confirmation (the product bought and a purchase token) so that it can give you the item. You can restore a purchase from the Settings screen. Refunds are handled by Google Play according to its refund policy.

6. Permissions the app uses

  • Internet and network state – to load ads, talk to Google Play and send analytics and crash reports.
  • Vibration – for optional haptic feedback (you can switch it off in Settings).
  • Google Play Billing – to make in-app purchases.
  • Advertising ID and ad-services permissions – used by the ads SDK as described in section 3. Firebase is set not to use the Advertising ID for advertising.
  • The Google software libraries built into the game add a few more permissions automatically. They are used only by those libraries, for the purposes in sections 3 to 5: keeping the device awake and running short background work so that an ad or an analytics upload can finish (the game itself does not run in the background); reading basic phone state (added by the Google Mobile Ads SDK); and checking where the game was installed from, for example the Play Store (the install-referrer permission, used by Firebase Analytics).

7. What we do not collect

The game does not ask for, access or collect: your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, files, microphone, camera, precise location or SMS messages. The game contains no social features, chat or user-generated content. The only analytics and crash-reporting service it uses is Google Firebase, described in section 4. If we add another service, we will update this policy first.

We do not sell your personal information and we do not use it to make decisions that have legal or similarly significant effects on you.

8. Children

Crash Drop is a general-audience game and is not directed to children under 13 (or under the minimum age of digital consent in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child has provided information, or you want ads personalisation turned off, please contact us and see the choices in section 3.

9. Keeping and deleting data

  • Data on your device stays until you delete it. You can erase it at any time with Android Settings → Apps → Crash Drop → Storage → Clear data, or by uninstalling the game. Doing so resets your progress; purchases can be restored from Google Play.
  • Ad-related data is kept by Google according to its own retention policies; see the links in section 3.
  • Analytics and crash data is kept by Google Firebase for a limited period set in our Firebase settings, and is not linked to your identity. You can stop it being sent at any time in Settings (section 4).
  • We do not run our own servers. The analytics and crash data in section 4 is stored by Google in our Firebase project under a random ID, so we cannot tell whose it is and cannot look it up by name. You can stop it being sent with the Settings switch, and reinstalling the game gives it a new random ID. If you think we hold information about you, write to us and we will respond.

10. Your rights and choices

Depending on where you live (for example the EEA, the United Kingdom, India, or certain US states such as California) you may have the right to access, correct, delete or port your personal data, to object to or restrict its processing, to withdraw consent, and to complain to your data-protection authority. Since we do not hold identifiable personal data about you, most requests are answered by the choices in sections 3, 4 and 9; for anything else contact us at itsharshkantgupta@gmail.com and we will reply as soon as we can.

Where we rely on consent (for personalised ads, analytics and crash reports), you can withdraw it at any time as described in sections 3 and 4.

11. Security and international transfers

Game data is kept in the app's private storage on your device, protected by Android. Network traffic between the ads, billing, analytics and crash-reporting services and your device is handled by Google using industry-standard encryption. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Google may process ad-related, analytics and crash data in countries other than your own, including the United States, under its own safeguards. We do not transfer personal data ourselves.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy, for example when we add features or services. The “Last updated” date at the top shows when it last changed. Material changes will also be reflected in the game or its store listing. Continued use after a change means you accept the updated policy.
